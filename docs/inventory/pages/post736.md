## Jet Ski Rental Requirements in Florida
To ensure safety and compliance with Florida law, all riders must meet the following requirements.
### How old do you have to be to rent a jet ski in Florida?
You must be at least 18 years old to rent a jet ski. Riders 16–17 years old may operate a jet ski with a parent or legal guardian present and signing the required paperwork. A safety briefing is provided before every ride.
### Do I need a boating license or Boater Safety ID?
Yes. Anyone born on or after January 1, 1988 is required by Florida law to have a Boater Safety Identification Card to operate a jet ski. If you don’t already have one, we’ll explain your options before your ride.
### Is fuel included in the rental price?
Yes — fuel is included. There are no surprise charges.
### Can beginners ride jet skis?
Absolutely. Our jet skis are beginner-friendly, and our team provides full instructions before you head out.
### What should I bring with me?
A valid photo ID, swimwear, sunscreen, and a sense of adventure.
