# Why Fort De Soto Is One of Florida’s Best Places to Ride a Jet Ski
Fort De Soto Park isn’t just another beach.
It’s considered one of the crown jewels of Florida’s Gulf Coast.
Surrounded by crystal-clear water, barrier islands, sandbars, and protected wildlife habitats, the area offers conditions that are ideal for jet skiing almost year-round.
When you launch near Fort De Soto, you’re immediately surrounded by:
- Wide-open riding areas
- Calm protected bays
- Beautiful beaches
- Gulf access
- Historic landmarks
- Dolphins
- Bird sanctuaries
- Stunning views of the Sunshine Skyway Bridge
Every direction offers something worth exploring.

# What You’ll Experience During Your Ride
Every tour is unique because wildlife, tides, and weather conditions change throughout the year.
Depending on your selected tour length, you may visit:
## Bunces Pass
One of the area’s favorite riding locations.
Known for:
- Deep blue water
- Strong tidal flow
- Excellent dolphin activity
- Incredible scenery
## Shell Key Preserve
Shell Key is one of Florida’s most beautiful undeveloped barrier islands.
Accessible only by boat, it’s famous for:
- White sand beaches
- Shell collecting
- Bird nesting areas
- Crystal-clear water
Many guests say this becomes the highlight of their trip.
## Egmont Key
Longer tours may include riding toward historic Egmont Key.
Here you’ll find:
- Historic lighthouse views
- Fort Dade ruins
- Beautiful beaches
- Snorkeling opportunities
- Frequent dolphin sightings
Few experiences compare to riding across open water toward this incredible island.
## Sunshine Skyway Bridge
You’ll enjoy spectacular views of one of Florida’s most recognizable landmarks.
Seeing the bridge from the water provides an entirely different perspective than driving across it.
# Dolphins Are One of Our Biggest Attractions
One question we hear almost every day is:
“Will we see dolphins?”
While wildlife can never be guaranteed, the waters surrounding Fort De Soto have one of the healthiest populations of bottlenose dolphins in the Tampa Bay region.
We frequently encounter dolphins during our tours.
Watching them surface alongside your WaveRunner is something you’ll never forget.
Our guides always maintain respectful distances so the animals remain comfortable in their natural environment.
