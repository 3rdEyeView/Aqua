About Comer's Landscaping

Design. Install. Maintain.

Comer's Landscaping in Columbia, SC provides reliable year-round landscaping services. Owned and operated by Colin Comer, we provide services that meet both commercial and residential needs. Our landscaping services include commercial grounds maintenance, residential lawn care, installation of sod, mulch, and gravel, hardscaping, irrigation installation and repair, landscape design and lighting, and erosion control.

When it comes to quality work, we are the first you should call.
