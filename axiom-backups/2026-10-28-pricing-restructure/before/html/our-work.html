Xtreme Jet Ski Watersports

Business Guided jet ski expeditions around Tampa Bay
Location St. Petersburg, Florida
The business

Xtreme takes riders out of St. Petersburg on guided jet ski expeditions to sandbars, hidden islands and dolphin spots, with Captain Mike leading the way.

What mattered

Someone looking at a jet ski tour wants three answers fast: are you open, is there a spot, and how do I book? The site had to answer all three before anyone scrolls.

What we built

A bright, high-energy site over a full-screen water backdrop. Opening hours, Check Availability & Book and Call Now come first, then Captain Mike's guided expedition, then Google reviews. Tours, boating license info and FAQs are one click away.

Skilled trades · Custom tile

The Tile Father

Business Owner-operated custom tile installation
Service area Tampa Bay, Pinellas, Sarasota & Manatee
The business

Lucas França runs The Tile Father and sets the tile himself: floors, kitchen backsplashes and custom feature work like marble inlay bands and hand-painted mural insets, across Tampa Bay.

What mattered

Craftsmanship deserves a website that looks as good as the finished work. People hiring for tile want to see real jobs, know exactly who will be in their home, and ask for an estimate without a runaround.

What we built

A calm, craftsmanship-first site that opens on a grid of Lucas's own project photos. Recent work lists the room, the location and the exact tile used, Lucas introduces himself as the one point of contact, and Get My Quote and Call Now appear from the first screen to the last.

Recent work reads like a job sheet: the room, where it is and the exact tile used, from real jobs Lucas has posted.
