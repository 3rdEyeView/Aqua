Landscape Design

Know the plan before we plant

A new landscape is an investment in your property, and you should know exactly what you’re getting before any work begins. Once your project is approved, we lay out a landscape plan with the beds, palms, stone, and lighting, and adjust it with you before installation starts.

Not sure where to start? Browse our gallery and show us what you like. We’ll use it as a starting point for your design.

  • A landscape plan for your property
  • Plant, palm, tree, and succulent selection
  • Sod, topsoil, mulch, rock, and decorative stone
  • Grading, landscape edging, and site preparation
  • Landscape lighting for palms, beds, driveways, and lanais

How It Works

How your project comes together

A clear process from your first inquiry, so every step is clear before it starts.

Free Estimate Request

Call, text, or send the estimate form and tell us about your property and what you would like done. You will hear back from us the same day. For a quicker response, call or text us.

On-Site Walkthrough

We meet you at the property, look over the space together, take measurements, and listen to how you want it to look and function. Bring your questions and ideas.

Plan & Proposal

You receive a clear scope of the work that will be done, so there are no surprises. We go over it with you and adjust it until it fits.

Build & Installation

With the plan approved, you can leave the rest to us. Our crew follows it closely, treats your property with care, and delivers exactly what we agreed on.

Property Maintenance

Once the work is finished, scheduled maintenance is available to keep your property looking the way it did on day one.
