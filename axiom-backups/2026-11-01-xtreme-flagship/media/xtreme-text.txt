THE XTREME DIFFERENCE

MOST RENTALS GIVE YOU A BUOY LINE. WE GIVE YOU THE GULF.

A lot of jet ski rentals keep you inside a small marked zone, or idling through no-wake water for half your hour. We launch you toward open water with a local guide who knows where the calm water, the sandbars and the dolphins are that day.

OPEN WATER, NOT A CIRCLE

Ride the Gulf of Mexico, Boca Ciega Bay and Tampa Bay. We set boundaries each morning around the wind and tide, so you get the best water that's safe to ride.

A GUIDE WHO KNOWS THESE WATERS

Shallow grass flats, oyster bars, busy channels and changing tides. Your guide handles the navigation so you can just ride.

BUILT AROUND YOUR GROUP

WHERE THE RIDE GOES

ISLANDS, PASSES AND OPEN GULF

No two tours are the same. Tide, wind and wildlife change daily, and your route depends on the tour you pick. These are the places our guests talk about most.

HISTORIC ISLAND

EGMONT KEY

A protected island refuge at the mouth of Tampa Bay. Historic lighthouse, Fort Dade ruins and turquoise shallows.

BOAT ACCESS ONLY

SHELL KEY PRESERVE

An undeveloped barrier island you can only reach by boat. White sand, shelling and shallow water.

DOLPHIN WATER

FORT DE SOTO & BUNCES PASS

Deep blue water, strong tidal flow and some of the best dolphin activity in Tampa Bay, with the Sunshine Skyway on the horizon.

KEEP YOUR EYES OPEN

DOLPHINS & WILDLIFE

Dolphins, manatees, rays, sea turtles and coastal birds all live out here. Wildlife is never guaranteed, but it's a regular part of the ride.

BEFORE YOU RIDE

FOUR THINGS TO KNOW

01

BOATER CARD

Born on or after January 1, 1988? Florida law requires a boater safety card to drive. The temporary one takes minutes online.

GET YOUR BOATER CARD

02

BRING YOUR ID

Renters must be 18+ with a valid government-issued photo ID. International guests can use a passport. No deposit needed.

03

ARRIVE 30 MINUTES EARLY

Your reservation time is your water time. Groups of 5 or more, plan on arriving an hour ahead.

04

WEATHER HAPPENS

We watch the marine forecast every day. If conditions are unsafe, you get a full refund or an easy reschedule.
