FAQ

Questions? We've got answers.

Straight answers about pricing, what's included and how it works, so you know exactly what you're signing up for.

Pricing & billing

How much does it cost?

Axiom Essentials is $297 a month and Axiom Growth is $597 a month. Both plans have a $0 setup fee. With Growth, your Google Ads advertising spend is separate and paid directly to Google. Optional add-ons and advanced marketing solutions are available by custom quote. Submitting the Get Started form doesn't charge you anything.

Is there a setup fee?

No. Essentials and Growth both have a $0 setup fee. There's no separate charge for designing or building your website; you simply pay your monthly plan.

When is the first payment due?

Your first month is due at signup, and your plan is billed monthly after that. Nothing is charged when you submit the Get Started form: we'll review your business, recommend a plan and walk you through the terms first.

Is the Google Ads budget included?

No. Google Ads advertising spend is not included in the $597 Growth subscription. You choose your advertising budget and pay it directly to Google through your Google Ads account. Your Growth subscription covers the setup and ongoing management of one standard Google Search advertising program.

Are SMS messaging fees included?

Usage-based charges for SMS, calls, and applicable communication services may be billed separately, as disclosed in the service agreement.

What happens if I cancel?

Essentials is a monthly subscription you can cancel anytime. Growth has a 3-month minimum commitment; its cancellation terms are set out in your service agreement. Your business, domain, brand, logo, customer data and the content you give us always stay yours. The website design and build are provided as part of your active subscription, as explained under Ownership below.

Why is it monthly instead of one big website payment?

Because a website isn't finished the day it launches. It needs hosting, security, software updates and changes as your business grows. Instead of charging thousands upfront and leaving all of that to you, we build your website and then keep hosting, maintaining, updating and supporting it for one monthly price.

What's included

What's included in Essentials?

Essentials ($297/month) includes a professionally designed, mobile-responsive website; website hosting, SSL and ongoing maintenance; website updates and technical support within our standard service scope; foundational on-page SEO; contact forms and lead capture; plus automated Google review requests, instant new-lead response and missed-call text-back automation. Automations are activated during onboarding.

What's included in Growth?

Growth ($597/month) includes everything in Essentials, plus the Axiom CRM & Lead Management Pipeline, advanced automated SMS and email follow-up sequences, appointment scheduling and reminders, and lead nurturing workflows (seven standardized business automations in total). It also includes Google Search Ads campaign setup and ongoing management, campaign performance monitoring and optimization, monthly advertising and lead performance reporting, and priority support.

Does Growth include Google Ads management?

Yes. Growth includes the setup and ongoing management of one standard Google Search advertising program, with campaign performance monitoring, optimization and monthly advertising and lead performance reporting. Additional campaigns, other advertising platforms, extensive creative work and custom marketing requirements are quoted separately. Your advertising spend is paid directly to Google and isn't included in the subscription.

What is the Axiom CRM?

Growth includes access to the Axiom CRM & Lead Management system, which we set up and manage for you. Organize customer inquiries, track opportunities, manage your sales pipeline, and automate follow-ups from one centralized dashboard. It runs on established, proven business software, so you get a dependable system without having to configure it yourself.

How do the automations work?

We set up standardized automations in the Axiom Automation Platform and connect them to your website forms and business phone line. For example, a new lead who fills out your form gets an instant response, a caller you miss gets a text back, and customers can receive a neutral invitation to leave a Google review. With Growth, leads also move through your Axiom CRM pipeline with automated follow-up, scheduling and reminders. SMS automations are activated only after the required carrier registration, approval and successful configuration, so timing varies by business; we'll confirm what's active for you during onboarding.

Do I need a separate phone number?

Usually, yes. Text messaging and missed-call text-back typically run on a dedicated business number that we set up during onboarding. We'll confirm exactly what your setup needs, including how it works alongside your existing number, before anything goes live.

Are results guaranteed?

No. We build and manage your website, your automations and, with Growth, your Google Ads campaign to give your business a strong foundation, but no one can honestly guarantee leads, sales, revenue, rankings or specific advertising results. Results depend on factors like your market, budget, competition and how quickly leads are followed up.

Can you update my website after it launches?

Yes. Standard updates are part of the service, like changing text, photos, hours, services or contact details. Bigger projects, like a complete redesign or new custom features, go beyond standard updates, and we'll talk those through with you first.

Will my website work on phones?

Yes. Every Axiom website is built to work on phones, tablets and computers. Most of your customers will see it on a phone first, so we design with that in mind.

Do you handle hosting, security and backups?

Yes. Managed hosting, SSL, security, software updates and regular backups are all included. You don't need to buy or manage any of it yourself.

Can you help with Google and local search?

Every website is built with local-search fundamentals: a clean structure, mobile-friendly pages and clear information about your services and service area. Nobody can honestly guarantee rankings, and we don't, but your site starts on a solid foundation.

Can you connect forms, reviews or booking tools?

Usually, yes. Contact and lead forms are included, and we can bring in things like your reviews or an existing booking tool when it makes sense for your business. Every platform is different, so we'll confirm what's possible for your setup.

Ownership

Who owns my domain and business content?

You do. Your business, domain, brand, logo, customer data and any content or materials you give us always belong to you.

Who owns the website?

The website design and build are provided by Axiom as part of your active subscription. That's what makes a professional website possible without a big upfront cost. Your domain, brand and content stay yours throughout.
