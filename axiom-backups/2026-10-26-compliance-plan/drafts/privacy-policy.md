# Privacy Policy

**Effective date:** [date of publication]
**Last updated:** [date]

This Privacy Policy explains how [Legal entity name, e.g. "Axiom Systems LLC"] ("Axiom Systems", "we", "us") collects,
uses and shares information when you visit axiomsystems.systems (the "Site"), contact us, or use our website management,
CRM, automated communication and marketing services (the "Services").

## 1. Information we collect
**Information you give us.** When you submit our Get Started form or contact us, we collect your name, business name,
email address, phone number, current website (optional), the details you share about your business, the plan you're
interested in, and your communication choices (for example, whether you agree to receive marketing text messages).
If you become a client, we also collect billing and account information and the content and materials you provide for
your website and campaigns.

**Information collected automatically.** When you use the Site, we and our service providers collect technical
information such as IP address, browser and device type, pages viewed, referring pages, and the time of your visit,
using cookies and similar technologies. This includes:
- **Google Analytics 4** to understand how the Site is used;
- **Google Ads** conversion tracking to measure whether our ads lead to inquiries;
- **Cloudflare Turnstile** to protect our forms from automated spam; <!-- only if Turnstile is adopted -->
- security and server logs kept by our hosting provider.

**Information from others.** We may receive information from advertising platforms (for example, which ad led to your
visit) and from people who refer you to us.

## 2. How we use information
We use information to:
- respond to your inquiry and contact you about it by phone, text message and email;
- schedule and confirm appointments and send reminders;
- provide, manage and improve the Services;
- send marketing messages where you have agreed to receive them or where the law otherwise allows;
- measure and improve our website and advertising;
- prevent spam, fraud and abuse, and keep our systems secure;
- comply with legal obligations and enforce our agreements.

## 3. Text messages (SMS)
If you give us your mobile number, we may send text messages related to your inquiry, appointments and the Services.
If you separately opt in, we may also send marketing text messages. Message frequency varies. Message and data rates may
apply. Reply **STOP** to opt out at any time and **HELP** for help, or contact us at [support email]. Consent to marketing
texts is not a condition of purchase.

**We do not sell, rent or share mobile phone numbers or SMS opt-in consent with third parties or affiliates for their
marketing or promotional purposes.** Text messaging originator opt-in data and consent are not shared with any third
parties, except service providers that deliver messages on our behalf.
