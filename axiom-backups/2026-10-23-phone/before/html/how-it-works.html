The process

Four stages. The last one keeps going.

Most website projects end at launch. With Axiom, launch is where the ongoing part of the relationship begins. Here's what happens at each stage, and what we'll need from you.

01
Stage 01 · Getting started

Tell us about your business

It starts with a conversation, not a pile of forms. We'll ask what you do, who your customers are, where you work and what you want people to do when they find you: call, request a quote, book or stop by.

What we do

Listen first and ask the right questions

Look at your current website, if you have one

Walk you through your site's scope and our terms before any work begins

What we need from you

Your business name, services and service area

Your logo and photos of your work, if you have them

How you want customers to reach you

A little of your time to answer questions

No logo or good photos yet? That's common. We'll talk it through together.

02
Stage 02 · Design and build

We design and build your website

We design a custom site around your business: your services, your photos, your service area and the way you talk to customers. Not a template with your name swapped in.

What we take care of

A custom design that fits your brand

Pages that look and work great on phones

Tap-to-call buttons and contact forms

Your services and service area laid out clearly

Local-search fundamentals built in

Your part: answer questions as they come up. Nothing technical.

bragspressurewashing.com

03
Stage 03 · Refine and launch

We refine it with you, then launch

You review a working version of your website on your own phone and computer, not a sketch. Tell us what you'd like changed in plain words and we'll refine it together. When you're happy with it, we take it live.

How feedback works

You see the real site before your customers do

Point out anything that doesn't feel right

No design or technical vocabulary needed

We make the changes and walk you through them

Launch day, handled

Hosting set up and ready

Your website connected and taken live

Forms and call buttons tested

Checked on phones, tablets and computers

04
Stage 04 · Every month after launch

We keep managing it

This is what makes Website as a Service different. Launch isn't the finish line, and you don't get handed a login and a goodbye. Axiom stays responsible for your website, month after month.

Hosting

Your website lives on secure, managed hosting. We set it up and keep watch over it.

Updates

The software behind your site needs regular updates. We install them and make sure nothing breaks.

Security

We keep your site protected, so it stays safe and trustworthy for your customers.

Backups

Regular backups, so your site can be restored if something ever goes wrong.

Changes

New service, new photos, new hours? Tell us what changed and we'll update your website.

Support

Something not looking right? You reach the people who built your site and know it.
