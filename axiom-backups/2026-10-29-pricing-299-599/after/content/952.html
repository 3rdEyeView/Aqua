Why pay monthly instead of buying a website once?

Because a website is never really finished. It needs hosting, software updates, security and changes as your business grows. With a one-time project, those costs and chores land on you after launch. With Axiom, they're part of the service, and someone is always responsible for your site.

What's the difference between Essentials and Growth?

Essentials ($299/month) is your professionally designed website with hosting, SSL, maintenance, updates, support and foundational on-page SEO, plus automated review requests, instant new-lead response and missed-call text-back. Growth ($599/month) adds access to the Axiom CRM, advanced lead follow-up automations, appointment scheduling and Google Search Ads setup and ongoing management with monthly reporting. Advertising spend is not included and is paid separately to Google and/or Meta.

Is there a setup fee or a contract?

There's no setup fee on either plan; your first month is due at signup. Essentials can be cancelled anytime. Growth has a 3-month minimum commitment, and its cancellation terms are set out in your service agreement. Submitting the form doesn't charge you anything.

Will my website look like a template?

No. Every site is designed for the business it belongs to. Compare Aqua Safari and Xtreme above: same industry, same coastline, completely different websites.

What if I need something changed?

Send us what you need, like new photos, a new service, updated hours or prices, and we'll make the update for you. You don't need to log in or learn anything.

I already have a website. Can you help?

Yes. We'll design a new site for your business and bring over the content that's worth keeping, so you're not starting from zero.

How long does it take to launch?

It depends on the size of your site and how quickly we get your content and feedback. We'll give you a realistic timeline once we've reviewed your business.

Do you only work with businesses in Florida?

No. We're based in St. Petersburg, Florida, in the Tampa Bay area, and work remotely with small businesses across the U.S. Everything from the first call to ongoing updates can happen by phone, email and video.
