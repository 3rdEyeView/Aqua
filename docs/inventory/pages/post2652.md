⭐ 5-Star Rated Jet Ski Tours in St. Petersburg

If you’re searching for jet ski rentals in St. Petersburg, FL, you’re in the right place.
The waters around Tampa Bay offer some of the most beautiful riding conditions in Florida — from barrier islands and white sand beaches to open Gulf cruising and dolphin sightings. Whether you’re visiting for vacation or you’re a local looking for something exciting to do, a guided jet ski tour is one of the best experiences you can have on the water.
At Xtreme Jet Ski Watersports, we specialize in premium guided tours to Shell Key and Egmont Key — two of the most scenic destinations in the region.
## Why St. Petersburg Is One of Florida’s Best Places to Ride
St. Pete offers:
- Protected bay waters for smooth riding
- Gulf access for open-water thrills
- Barrier islands with crystal-clear shallows
- Frequent dolphin and wildlife sightings
- Warm weather most of the year
Few places in Florida offer this mix of scenery and ride conditions in one location.
## Shell Key Jet Ski Tours
Shell Key Preserve is a pristine barrier island just south of St. Pete Beach. It’s known for:
- White sand beaches
- Shallow turquoise water
- Incredible shelling
- Peaceful, undeveloped shoreline
Our guided Shell Key tours let you ride along the coastline, explore the sandbars, and stop for photos in some of the clearest water in the area.
This is perfect for:
- First-time riders
- Couples
- Families
- Visitors wanting calm water + great views
## Egmont Key Jet Ski Tours
Egmont Key State Park sits at the mouth of Tampa Bay where the Gulf meets the bay.
This tour offers:
- Lighthouse views
- Historic fort ruins
- Open-water riding
- Frequent dolphin sightings
- A more adventurous experience
If you want a little more speed and space, this is the tour for you.

## Is It Safe for Beginners?
Yes.

## When Is the Best Time to Book?
Spring Break through late summer is peak season in St. Petersburg. Weekends fill quickly.
For the best experience:
- Morning rides = smoother water
- Midday = warmer temps
- Sunset = best lighting for photos
If you’re planning ahead, booking early guarantees your time slot.
