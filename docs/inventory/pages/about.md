# About Us
## About Xtreme Jetski Watersports
More Than a Jet Ski Rental — It’s the Way Tampa Bay Is Meant to Be Experienced
Xtreme Jetski Watersports was built on a simple idea: jet ski rentals should be safe, stress-free, and genuinely fun — not rushed, restricted, or full of hidden fees. As a family-owned and operated business serving Pinellas County, we take pride in delivering a more personal, higher-quality experience on the water.
We’re locals who know Tampa Bay – The intercoastal waterways and Gulf Of Mexico inside and out, and we’ve spent years watching riders leave disappointed after being confined to small riding zones, surprised by extra charges, or handed outdated equipment. We knew there was a better way — so we created it.
### Our Approach
At Xtreme Jetski Watersports, every ride is tailored to the guest. Whether it’s your first time on a jet ski or you’re an experienced rider, we take the time to make sure you feel confident, comfortable, and excited before you ever hit the throttle.
Safety is always our top priority. We operate modern Yamaha WaveRunners, provide brand-new personal flotation devices for every rider, and include complimentary orientation classes to ensure you understand both the equipment and the riding area before launching.
### What Sets Us Apart
- No passenger fees — ever
- Unrestricted riding areas (no tiny circles or excessive no-wake zones)
- New, well-maintained Yamaha WaveRunners
- Brand-new life jackets for every guest
- Plenty of availability, even during peak season
- Personalized service, not a corporate assembly line
### Convenience & Locations
We operate 7 days a week, from 9 AM to 5 PM, with multiple locations throughout Pinellas County – with locations that provide easy access to Tampa Bay’s and the Gulf of Mexico best riding areas.
### Our Promise
We never rush our guests, we never surprise them with hidden costs, and we never cut corners on safety or equipment. Reservations are recommended — especially during the busy season from March through September — but we always do our best to accommodate every rider.
When you choose Xtreme Jetski Watersports, you’re choosing experience, integrity, and a team that genuinely cares about making your day on Tampa Bay unforgettable.
If you have any questions, feel free to reach out by phone, text, or email — we’re always happy to help and look forward to getting you out on the water.
