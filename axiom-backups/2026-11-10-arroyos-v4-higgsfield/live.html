How It Works

Our 5-step approach

A clear process from your first inquiry, so every step is clear before it starts.

01

Free Estimate Request

Call, text, or send the estimate form and tell us about your property and what you would like done. You will hear back from us the same day. For a quicker response, call or text us.

02

On-Site Walkthrough

We meet you at the property, look over the space together, take measurements, and listen to how you want it to look and function. Bring your questions and ideas.

03

Plan & Proposal

You receive a clear scope of the work that will be done, so there are no surprises. We go over it with you and adjust it until it fits.

04

Build & Installation

With the plan approved, you can leave the rest to us. Our crew follows it closely, treats your property with care, and delivers exactly what we agreed on.

05

Property Maintenance

Once the work is finished, scheduled maintenance is available to keep your property looking the way it did on day one.

Questions

Good to know

Straight answers to the questions customers ask us most.

How do I get an estimate, and is it free?
+

Estimates are free. Call or text (941) 962-1124, or send the request form with a few details about the property and the work you need. We reply the same day and set up a time that works for you to see the property in person before giving you a scope of work.

What areas do you serve?
+

We serve Bradenton, Lakewood Ranch, Sarasota, and nearby communities across Manatee and Sarasota counties. If your property is close by and you are not sure, call us and we will let you know.

Do you work with HOAs and property managers?
+

Yes. We maintain common areas, entrances, medians, and amenity areas for HOAs and property managers. We also handle irrigation repairs, palm and tree trimming, and storm cleanup, so the community works with one contractor instead of several.

Are you licensed and insured?
+

Yes. Arroyo’s Lawn Care LLC is licensed and fully insured for both residential and commercial work.

What happens after I send a request?
+

We reply the same day to confirm what you need and schedule a walkthrough at your property. After the walkthrough you receive a clear scope of the work, and once you approve it, we put the job on the schedule.
